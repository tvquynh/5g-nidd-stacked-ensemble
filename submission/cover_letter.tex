\textbf{Cover letter --- ATC 2026, Special Session \textit{AI-Enabled 5G/6G Communication Systems}}

\bigskip
\noindent
Dear Editors of the 2026 International Conference on Advanced Technologies
for Communications (ATC~2026),

\bigskip

We are pleased to submit our manuscript ``Lightweight Stacked Ensemble for
AI-Enabled 5G Network Intrusion Detection: A Latency--Accuracy Pareto
Analysis'' for consideration at ATC~2026, with a preferred placement in
the Special Session \emph{AI-Enabled 5G/6G Communication Systems}.

\paragraph{Topical fit.} The manuscript addresses a problem that sits
squarely inside the special session's scope: deploying AI-driven network
intrusion detection on the 5G access and edge plane under the latency
constraints that govern in-line classification. We use the public
5G-NIDD benchmark---a 1.2-million-flow capture from a real 5G test
network covering eight attack families across two base stations---to
characterise both the classification quality and the latency--accuracy
trade-off of a heterogeneous shallow stacked ensemble.

\paragraph{Contributions.} The manuscript reports three contributions.
First, we propose a heterogeneous shallow stacked ensemble that combines
LightGBM, XGBoost, and a two-layer MLP through leakage-free out-of-fold
probability stacking with a logistic-regression meta-learner. Second, we
evaluate the ensemble under both a stratified random split and a
cross-station split (BS1$\rightarrow$BS2) that rebalances the
per-class prior to isolate true generalisation across two physically
distinct radio cells. Third, we trace the latency--accuracy Pareto
frontier on a commodity 60-core CPU host across six batch sizes
$\{1, 16, 64, 256, 1024, 4096\}$ and publish a three-tier deployment
recommendation table mapping operating points (latency budget, accuracy
floor) to the configuration that meets them.

\paragraph{Originality and scope.} The submission is original, has not
been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by any other
venue. All four authors have read and approved the manuscript. The
artefact (code, configuration, ten project seeds, and aggregation
pipeline) is reviewer-runnable from a single command and is released
under MIT, with a Zenodo DOI to be minted upon acceptance.

\paragraph{Suggested topics (ATC tracks).} The work intersects the
\emph{AI-Enabled 5G/6G Communication Systems} special session and the
\emph{Network Operations and Management} main track. We respectfully
request consideration for the special session as the primary fit.
